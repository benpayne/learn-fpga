# Golden reference: Q8_0 quantized stories260K generation (T010)

Generated by `FemtoRV/FIRMWARE/llama2/tools/runq_host.c`, the host-side
byte-for-byte port of upstream karpathy/llama2.c's runq.c. This is THE
definition of correct for every later verification stage (simulation,
standalone accelerator test, end-to-end firmware run) per
specs/004-int8-matmul-accel/contracts/build-and-verify.md.

## Command

```
cd FemtoRV/FIRMWARE/llama2/tools
gcc -O2 -o runq_host runq_host.c -lm
./runq_host model.q8.bin tokenizer.bin --seed 2026 --steps 110 -i "Once upon a time"
```

## Comparison against the fp32 baseline (feature 003)

For reference, the unquantized fp32 model with the same prompt produces:

```
Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a jolly apple that she loved to
play outside. One day, she went to the park with her mom. She saw a big box
```

The Q8_0 output is coherent English and matches the fp32 text closely
through "She saw a big " before diverging (tree vs. box) -- this is the
kind of divergence quantization is expected to produce, not the garbage
that a broken conversion would. See research.md R15 for the
group-size story: GS=64 (the naively "expected" value) produced degenerate
repeated-token garbage on THIS model; GS=4 (correctly backed off) produces
the coherent text above.
